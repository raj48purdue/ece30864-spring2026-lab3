Question 1 -
Agile pros:
    Adapts to changes easily
    Delivers the features quite fast in small chunks
Agile cons:
    Hard to predict exact time and cost
    Needs a lot of feedback and constant communication

Non Agile pros:
    There is a clear plan and documentation upfront
    It is much easier to estimate schedule and hence budget early on in the project
Non-Agile cons:
    Any changes made during are quite expensive and also very slow
    As testing and feedback happens later, you will find problems late during the project timeline

Question 2 -
Scrum Master: - Keeps the team on the Scrum process and runs meetings 
              - Helps remove blockers so the team can keep working
Product Owner: - Decides what to build next and sets priorities in the backlog
               - Explains requirements and accepts the work when it is done

Question 3 -
Common meetings:
    - Sprint planning
        - Pick what backlog stories to go into the sprint and set a clear goal
        - Break stories into tasks amd confirm who works on what
    - Daily Standup
        - What you did since the last standup and what you will do next
        - Any issues slowing you down
    - Sprint Review
        - Show what was finished and get feeback from the team
        - Confirm what is done, what did not finish, and what changes came up

Question 4 - 
    Epic: A big piece of work that’s broken into smaller stories or tasks
    Story: A small feature or requirement that delivers value to the user
    Task: A simple piece of work that needs to be done (not user-focused)
    Bug: Something that’s broken and needs fixing
